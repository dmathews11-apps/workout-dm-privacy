
Workout DM Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-11-02

Workout DM is an iPhone and Apple Watch workout app. The developer runs no server and receives nothing from the app. It has no accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.

What the app uses
Read from Apple Health, with your permission. iPhone: heart rate, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability and recovery, VO2 max, respiratory rate, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep, body weight, active and resting energy, walking and running distance, steps, running speed, power, stride length, vertical oscillation, ground contact time, workouts (including other apps' workouts and notes, such as Caddie DM rounds), workout routes (GPS locations), date of birth and biological sex. Watch: workouts, heart rate, active and resting energy.

Saved to Apple Health. When the watch records a workout itself, it saves that workout with Apple's measurements, each set (exercise, reps, weight or hold time, times) and set totals. The iPhone app saves nothing to Health.

Wrist movement. While the watch records a workout, it records wrist motion and sends it to the iPhone.

What you enter. Sessions and sets, saved workouts, goals, custom exercises, starting weights, golf answers, an imported Fitbod file, and any typed age, sex, weight and height.

Use. Only to show and analyze your training.

Where it is stored
iPhone and watch. In the app's own files, including an event log kept 60 days.

Your iCloud Drive. When iCloud Drive is on for the app, backups go automatically to a "Workout DM Backups" folder. They hold sessions and sets, saved workouts, settings, goals, starting weights, custom exercises, machine settings, planned sessions, the prompt log, golf answers, Last.fm songs, and identifiers linking sessions to Health workouts. They hold no heart rate, sleep, energy, route or other Apple Health sample, no wrist movement, and no typed age, sex, weight or height.

Apple's device backup. An iPhone backup to iCloud or a computer can include the app's files and settings, such as logged sets, typed age, sex, weight and height, and an event log holding some heart rate and energy readings. The app excludes its stored heart rate estimates and wrist movement recordings from these backups. While an OneDrive upload is under way, a copy of the export or a wrist-movement file, or part of one, can also be included.

Exports. Settings can build one file: stored sessions, the Health data around them, daily metrics, GPS routes, calculations, settings, event log and device details. It stays on the iPhone until you share it, pick an export folder or sign in to OneDrive. A folder then receives it automatically, with wrist-movement files. If you choose iCloud Drive, Health data goes there by your choice.

Optional services
OneDrive. Off until you sign in. Exports and the newest wrist-movement files then upload automatically to your own account, in Documents/Claude/Workout App/data, and older automatic copies there are deleted. The app reads your account name and uses only that folder.

Last.fm. Off until you enter your own username and API key. The app sends them with each workout's time window, plus artist and title to look up track lengths. No health data is sent.

Route maps use Apple's MapKit. The app does not access photos or ask for location; routes come only from Health.

Sharing
The developer shares your data with nobody. A third-party service receives data only after you sign in or enter your key, for your own account. Health data is never sold or used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

TestFlight. Apple gives the developer crash reports, feedback you choose to send, and basic tester and usage details, under Apple's own terms.

How long it is kept
Until you delete it. Backups are thinned with age to the oldest and newest of each week, then of each month. A deleted session keeps a safety copy on the iPhone until the app is deleted, and stays in older backups.

Deleting data and withdrawing permission
Devices: delete the app from iPhone and watch.
Backups: remove "Workout DM Backups" from iCloud Drive in Files. Turn off iCloud Drive for the app in iPhone Settings.
Health: turn off access in Health's settings. Saved workouts stay there until deleted in Health.
Wrist movement: turn off "Record wrist movement" in Settings.
OneDrive: "Sign out of OneDrive" in Settings. Uploaded files stay.
Last.fm: "Remove key" in Settings.
Children
Workout DM is not directed at children under 13.
